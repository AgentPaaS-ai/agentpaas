# What Athena can do

<!-- COPY TWIN: docs-site/docs/trial/what-athena-can-do.md. Change both or neither. -->

Athena is the console assistant for your AgentPaaS Cloud tenancy. She is read-only. She has her own instance for your tenancy. She is never shared with another customer.

This page is also linked from console Settings, Athena.

## What Athena can do

- Answer how-to questions from the baked-in product docs.
- See **your** tenancy only: deployments, runs, run detail, workflows, audit trail, usage, and secret **names** (never values).
- Look up any run, deployment, workflow, or audit entry and link you straight to the console page for it.
- Remember your preferences across sessions (per user). Clear them in Settings, Athena.
- Guide the first-run demo and console walkthrough (three-visit pattern).
- Sleep after 2 minutes idle, then wake on your next message. Yours alone. Never shared with another tenant.

## What Athena cannot do

- Cannot change anything: no deploys, no deletes, no invokes, no secret writes. Read-only is enforced by the token, not by the prompt.
- Cannot see other tenants. Ever.
- Cannot fetch the live internet. Egress is locked to the AgentPaaS API and the model endpoint.
